How to play: You are BLUE (bottom) and race to the top blue point. The computer is RED and moves first. Click one of your balls, then click each hole it goes to. A move is one step to a next-door hole, or a chain of hops over single balls. After a hop, click the next landing hole, or press Done (or click the ball again) to stop. First to fill their target point wins.
